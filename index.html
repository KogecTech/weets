About WEETS

WEETS is an annual event that generates new ideas for encouraging women and girls, from the girl-child stage to the corporate woman, to take outstanding roles in closing the technology gender gap.

WEETS 2024 is a platform that highlights opportunities for women and girls by bridging the digital divide and expanding new ideas on ICT and telecom, positioning them to play a formidable role in the Nigerian tech space. It also recognizes and celebrates the women and the corporate organizations already blazing the trail in Nigeria's ICT ecosystem.

The objectives of WEETS 2024

The summit gives women across sectors a dynamic platform to explore the intersection of AI and leadership, share insights, experiences and best practices, and chart a course for gender diversity and inclusion in tech.

It features keynote presentations, panel discussions, workshops and networking, covering these topics:

  • The role of AI in shaping the future of business leadership and innovation.
  • Strategies for fostering diversity, equity, and inclusion in AI development and implementation.
  • Overcoming challenges and barriers to advancement as women leaders in the tech industry.
  • Leveraging AI technologies for business growth, sustainability, and societal impact.
  • Empowering the next generation of women and girls to excel in AI and technology fields.

We believe live events bring people together, stimulate discussion and debate, and give networking a home. A virtual component extends our reach and engagement.
